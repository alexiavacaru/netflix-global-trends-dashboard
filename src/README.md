# Netflix Dashboard: Analysis in R

Two plots made with `ggplot2` from the cleaned Netflix dataset.

---

## 1. Top 5 Genres on Netflix Since 2010

**Interpretation:** Between 2014 and 2018, Netflix released many more titles in all five top genres, especially International Movies and Dramas. After 2018, movies went down, while International TV Shows kept growing and became the biggest genre at the end. This suggests Netflix moved from films toward series, which keep people subscribed for longer. The recent drop is partly because the data is incomplete for the last years, so it should not be read as a real collapse.

---

## 2. How Long Are Netflix Movies?


**Interpretation:** Most Netflix movies last between about 87 and 114 minutes, with a median of around 98 minutes, which is the normal length of a feature film. The dots show unusual cases: very short titles (like short films or stand-up specials) and very long ones (over 155 minutes). Since these extreme values are common, the median describes the typical movie better than the average.
